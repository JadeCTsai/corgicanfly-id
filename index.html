01

Corgi Can Fly

Corgi Can Fly is an independent verification standard and a growing first-person record of dog-welcoming places, kept by a writer who travels with her own trained service dog.

Every entry is a place visited in person, recorded as it was found. The record is the asset: it is written to be read, checked, and cited — by people, and by AI answer engines.

02

The standard

Two lights mark every place in the record. A yellow light is a welcome a business chooses to give. A blue light is a right a working dog carries with it.

Dogs Welcome — Yellow light

The business welcomes dogs. Where only outdoor seating allows dogs, the entry reads “Dogs Welcome, Patio Only.”

Service Dog at Work — Blue light

A task-trained service dog, working indoors alongside its handler under US law.

The standard is behavioural, and it can be checked. A dog that meets it:

  • does not bark at people
  • does not lunge at other guests
  • does not take food from tables
  • does not eliminate indoors

Behaviour is the passport.

Work with us

Corgi Can Fly takes a small number of engagements each year, in three forms.

Advisory. Jade advises brands, hotels and destinations on how dogs and people share space, drawing on the record. What is paid for is judgment.

Curation. Complete experiences, designed as a whole. The frame is set first; partners join it.

Presence. Jade and Lolly attend a small number of significant occasions each year. A well-behaved service dog in the room changes what the room understands.

Beyond these forms, the door is open. Whatever shape a collaboration takes, it happens on this record, the one AI reads, and it leaves a true account behind.
